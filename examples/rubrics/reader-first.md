# Reader-first rubric

Score the writing for a busy reader who will act on it.

- **Lead**: the first sentence carries the most important thing for this reader.
- **Length**: nothing the reader does not need. No preamble, no recap.
- **Plain words**: concrete, specific, no filler, no hedging that the facts do not require.
- **Accuracy**: every claim matches the source material. Nothing important is dropped or invented.
- **Form**: fits where it will be read (a Slack message is not a report).

Accuracy failures cap the score at 4. A correct but padded or buried result scores 5 to 7.
