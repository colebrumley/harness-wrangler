# Placebo corpus

Text for placebo overlays: instructions that restate what an agent does
anyway, so a placebo of matched length measures "having instructions at
all" and nothing else. One sentence per line under "Sentences", one
heading per line under "Headings". Lines starting with `#` or blank are
ignored inside those lists.

A sentence belongs here only if deleting it could not plausibly change an
agent's output. That rules out anything about quality, length, tone, care,
checking, testing, planning, asking, or order of work.
